The house of stone

Reserved in its entirety for one party at a time

Pietra Latra was a working farm, and later a small hotel, on a ridge eight minutes from Casole d'Elsa. Everyone in the valley knows it. Rebuilt from the ground in 2026–27, it is now a private estate for up to 20 guests: a main house of six en-suite bedrooms, a second house of two family duplexes, and 29 hectares of olive grove and open hillside looking south over the soft hills of Tuscany.

The house runs on its own solar power, is gated and quietly monitored, and comes with a property manager on call around the clock. It is self-catered by design — a kitchen built for professional chefs — and everything else, from a private cook to a driver to a daily maid, can be arranged before you arrive.

The main house

Six bedrooms upstairs, all with the view

Every bedroom sits on the first floor and looks out over the hills. Four are large — two with private balconies — and two are a touch smaller. Each has its own en-suite bathroom; a seventh bathroom serves the ground floor. Beds are 180 or 200 cm wide and made up with fresh linen.

  • Dining room and a great hall
  • Large sitting room
  • Media and games room with consoles
  • Wine cellar, stocked and for sale
  • Sound system in every ground-floor room
  • Air conditioning throughout
  • Pergola wrapped around the whole house
  • Starlink Wi-Fi indoors and out

A kitchen for chefs

Two of everything — ovens, sinks, dishwashers — so a private chef can work at full pace. A separate scullery kitchen takes the heavy machinery, and a laundry room the washing.

Room for all of you

Indoors and out, there are tables and seats for the whole party to eat together — in the dining room, under the pergola, or around the fire pit at night.

The second house

Two duplexes, each for two adults and two children with its own bathroom — and the finest view on the estate. Below them, the gym.

The estate

Twenty-nine hectares, seventy-five old olive trees

The land drops away from the house in every direction. There is an olive grove that produces the estate's own oil, a boules court, an outdoor kitchen with separate grills for meat and vegetables, and a fire pit with tables and chairs. Parking for as many cars as you bring, behind a gated entrance.

  • Estate olive oil to taste and buy
  • Outdoor kitchen and two grills
  • Fire pit with seating
  • Boules court
  • Gated entrance, alarm and CCTV on request
  • Powered entirely by the estate's solar panels
Wellness & sport

A pool that is warm in February

The 5 × 10 metre infinity pool is heated and can be kept open the whole year. It is edged in travertine and lavender, with loungers for sixteen. Beneath it, a large sauna and an outdoor shower. Beneath the second house, a 4 × 6 metre gym fitted with the latest TechnoGym equipment. And beside the house, a new padel court with everything you need to play.

  • Heated infinity pool, open year-round on request
  • Large sauna and outdoor shower
  • TechnoGym fitness room
  • Padel court with rackets and balls
  • Ceramics studio — lessons by arrangement
  • Mountain-bike trails from the gate

Experiences

Tuscany, arranged from the house

Truffle hunting

Location

Between Siena, Volterra and San Gimignano

Casole d'Elsa is a walled hill town of 3,800 people, eight minutes down the road: good restaurants and wine bars, two butchers, a small market. A larger supermarket is twenty minutes away. The road to the house is a well-kept gravel lane — no four-wheel drive needed.

Celebrations

Weddings and gatherings on the hill
